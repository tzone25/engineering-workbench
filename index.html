Hydraulic Fault-Finding Guide
No pressure
Check oil level, pump drive, pump rotation, inlet restriction, relief valve condition and control/valve configuration.
Pressure low under load
Compare commanded pressure with measured pressure. Investigate relief valve, pump condition, internal leakage and system demand.
Slow cylinder
Check actual flow, restrictions, valve opening, pump output, hose condition and cylinder leakage.
Hydraulic overheating
Investigate excessive pressure drop, relief-valve bypassing, internal leakage, cooler performance and oil condition.
High case drain
Compare measured case drain with the manufacturer's allowable specification before condemning a pump or motor.

Engineering Reference

Quick workshop reference.

⚙️ Mechanical

Force = mass × acceleration
Power = torque × angular velocity
Stress = force ÷ area
Velocity = distance ÷ time

💧 Hydraulics

Hydraulic power (kW) ≈ pressure (bar) × flow (L/min) ÷ 600
Force = pressure × area

🔩 Machining

RPM = cutting speed × 1000 ÷ (π × diameter)
Milling feed = RPM × teeth × feed/tooth
