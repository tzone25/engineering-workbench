⚠️ Hydraulic Safety

Treat hydraulic systems as stored-energy systems. Identify pressure, isolate where required, relieve stored energy and verify the safe condition before mechanical work.

⚙️ Bearings

Check lubrication, temperature, alignment, loading, contamination, installation condition and abnormal noise/vibration.

🔩 Shafts

Consider torsion, bending, stress concentration, keyways, shoulders, material and fatigue.

💧 Hydraulic Heat

Investigate excessive pressure drop, relief valve operation, leakage, restriction, incorrect flow and cooling performance.

📐 Engineering Drawings

Check revision, units, material, dimensions, tolerances, surface finish, GD&T and notes before manufacture.
